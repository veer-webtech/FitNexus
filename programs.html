Curriculum Syllabus

Hypertrophy Protocol X

Periodized week-by-week curriculum overview.

Phase 1: Volume Accumulation (Weeks 1-4) RPE 7-8

Primary compound progression with 12-16 weekly sets per muscle group. High focus on time-under-tension and eccentric control.

Phase 2: Intensification & Heavy Load (Weeks 5-8) RPE 8.5-9.5

Load ramps to 82-88% 1RM. Rest intervals widen to 3.5 minutes for full ATP replenishment. Biometric recovery tracking actively engaged.

Phase 3: Peak Hypertrophy & Supercompensation (Weeks 9-12) Max Strain

Myo-reps, drop sets, and lengthened partials applied to isolate motor units, followed by a scheduled 5-day active recovery deload.
