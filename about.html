Our Manifesto

Fitness is Not an Opinion.
It is Applied Physiology.

For decades, the fitness industry relied on bro-science, generic templates, and emotional marketing. KINETIQ was founded by biomechanists and artificial intelligence researchers to replace superstition with measurable empirical truth.

The Origin

Born in High-Performance Labs. Scaled for You.

In 2023, our founders were working with elite Olympic sprinters and powerlifters. We observed a constant paradox: athletes possessed \$1,000 smart rings and \$800 GPS watches, yet their coaching programs were still static spreadsheets created 8 weeks prior.

When an athlete slept poorly or had an immune response, the spreadsheet didn't care. Injuries happened, progress stalled. We built KINETIQ to close the feedback loop between biological telemetry and training programming.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

01. Truth in Data

We do not engage in fitness folklore or marketing hype. If a protocol does not show statistically significant positive adaptations in clinical trials, it does not enter our platform.

02. Respect the Nervous System

More is not better; better is better. We prioritize central nervous system recovery over mindless fatigue, ensuring lifelong durability without catastrophic injuries.

03. Uncompromising Craft

Software should be exhilarating to use. Every animation, metric, chart, and response in KINETIQ is designed to feel like luxury aerospace engineering.
