Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Direct answers to how FitNexus operates, data privacy, and wearable hardware integration.

How does FitNexus differ from generic workout apps?
↓

Generic apps provide static, one-size-fits-all routines that ignore whether your nervous system is fresh or fatigued. FitNexus syncs directly with your wearable biometrics (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep architecture, recovery scores) to dynamically recalibrate volume, intensity, and rest periods in real-time.

Which wearables and health platforms are supported?
↓

FitNexus natively supports Apple Health, Apple Watch, Whoop 4.0, Oura Ring Gen 3, Garmin Connect, and Polar. We also integrate with Concept2 ergometers and smart barbells.

Can I train if I don't own a wearable sensor?
↓

Yes. While wearable bio-telemetry unlocks automated adjustments, FitNexus includes an intelligent subjective readiness check-in that assesses soreness, sleep quality, and perceived exertion (RPE) to customize your daily sessions.

Is my health and biometric data kept secure and private?
↓

Your privacy is absolute. All health telemetry is encrypted end-to-end with AES-256 and stored in HIPAA-compliant, SOC2 Type II audited vaults. We never sell, license, or share your biometric data with third-party advertisers or insurance companies.

Can I cancel my membership if I change my mind?
↓

Yes, you can cancel in one click directly from your dashboard settings with no hidden cancellation fees. We also offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee if you are not completely satisfied.
